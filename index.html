The whole triptych

You've now examined Nativity with the Prophets Isaiah and Ezekiel (c. 1308–1311) panel by panel. This is itself a predella panel from Duccio's Maestà altarpiece for Siena Cathedral, framed as its own miniature triptych — now in the National Gallery of Art.

Now that all three panels sit together: how does the whole change your reading of each part? What did the frame withhold from you while you looked at only one piece — and what does that withholding tell you about how this painting was designed to be read?

National Gallery of Art, Washington · Samuel H. Kress Collection

The National Gallery of Art is the only institution in the United States to own two panels from Duccio's Maestà. Read more about the work here: https://www.nga.gov/artworks/10-nativity-prophets-isaiah-and-ezekiel.
